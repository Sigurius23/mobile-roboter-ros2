Here’s why the guess matters, on real scans. The robot turns on the spot, almost fifty degrees within half a second. Started at zero, most points are more than a metre from any partner. Only nine pairs survive the one-metre limit, and ICP returns nonsense. Started at the filter’s guess, it lines up within a few iterations, with a hundred and ninety seven pairs, and the turn matches the wheels: minus 0.837 radians against minus 0.838. ICP only finds the nearest alignment, so where it starts matters. Always starting at zero fails on fast turns and large gaps.
