Six questions

What does getTransform return, and which way?

A rotation and shift from the sensor frame into base_footprint, or false.

Why is the guess sideways zero?

Wheeled robots can’t slide sideways.

Why invert the guess and the result?

ICP moves points, which move opposite to the robot.

Why R = V·Uᵀ, and what if det R < 0?

Best least-squares rotation; flip V’s last column.

Why does ICP enter the filter as a speed?

It measures a change between scans, not a position.

Which three things were fixed?

Loop reset, ICP noise (no √N, σ_ω / dt), /imu/data_raw.

Pause on each question and answer it out loud before revealing the answer; each click shows the next one. getTransform returns a rotation and shift from the sensor frame into base_footprint, or false. The guess is sideways zero because wheeled robots can’t slide sideways. We invert the guess and the result because ICP moves points, and points move opposite to the robot. R = V Uᵀ is the best least-squares rotation, and if its determinant is negative you flip the last column of V. ICP enters the filter as a speed because it measures a change between scans, not a position. The three fixes: the reset when the bag loops, the ICP noise formula, and the IMU topic.
