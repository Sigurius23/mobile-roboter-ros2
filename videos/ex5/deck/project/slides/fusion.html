WHY AT LEAST 10 PAIRS

WHY A SPEED

ICP measures a change between two scans, not a position. Divided by dt it’s the same kind of measurement as the wheels, so the same H rows work.

ICP’s result goes into the Kalman filter, still inside scanCallback, right after TASK 3. This block runs only when use_icp is on and icp_only is off. First a check: at least ten pairs. With fewer, the match isn’t trustworthy and the scan isn’t used. ICP measured a change between two scans. Divided by dt, the forward shift becomes a speed and the turn becomes a turn rate. That’s the same kind of measurement as the wheels, so the filter can use the same H rows: row zero measures state entry three, the speed, and row one measures entry four, the turn rate. Testat question: why does ICP enter as a speed? Because it measures a change between scans, not an absolute pose.
