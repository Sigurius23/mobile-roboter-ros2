Here is the node running on the real bag, seen from above; this is the last frame of the recording in the Part 5 video. The teal points are the lidar scans. RViz keeps each scan on screen for a minute, so together they draw a map of the field. The orange line is the robot’s path: the transform odom to base_footprint that our node publishes. The two small sets of axes are the frames odom, where the robot started, and base_footprint, the robot itself. Watch the walls and the fences: every scan is drawn where our node says the robot was. If the motion estimate were wrong, the same wall would show up twice, shifted or smeared. Here the walls stay mostly sharp, so the motion and the scans agree.
