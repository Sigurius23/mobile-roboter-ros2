TASK 3 · THE IDEA

Pair, align, move, and again

  1. Pair every old point with its nearest new point.
  2. Compute the one rotation and shift that brings the pairs closest together.
  3. Move the old scan by it.
  4. Repeat until the step is tiny.
  5. The total of all steps is the robot’s motion.

Here’s the idea on two real scans, half a second apart. The robot drove forward, so the same walls show up in slightly different places. ICP pairs every old point with the nearest new point. Then it computes the one rotation and shift that brings the pairs closest together, and moves the old scan by it. Then it pairs again and moves again, until the step is tiny. The total of all steps is the robot’s motion: 0.68 metres forward. The wheels say 0.63.
